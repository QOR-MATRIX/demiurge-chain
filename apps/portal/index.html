One identity, every world.

A QOR ID is a single name that travels with you across every world built on Demiurge — readable by people, resolvable by machines. It is issued once, as name#0001, and it never changes.

Your name
Three to twenty characters, letters, digits and underscores. The chain appends the discriminator — alaustrup#0001.
Your record
Every QOR ID resolves to a decentralised identifier, did:demiurge:…, and an on-chain address that holds your CGT and your DRC-369 assets.
Your key
Prefer no password at all? Authenticate with an Ed25519 keypair by signing a one-time challenge. The same path autonomous agents use.
